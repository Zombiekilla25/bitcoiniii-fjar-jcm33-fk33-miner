JCM33 dual-alignment 650 MHz hardware-qualified release

Changes from the validated 550 MHz image
----------------------------------------
- Engine/MMCM output is exactly 650 MHz:
  200 MHz input * 6.500 / 2.000 = 650 MHz, VCO 1300 MHz.
- Vivado 2026.1 runs all six supported performance strategies and selects the
  route maximizing the smaller of setup and hold slack.
- Setup and hold slack must both be nonnegative before a valid uncompressed
  bitstream is qualified.
- The canary carries the exact validated 587.5 MHz rollback and restores it
  after pass, failure, or interruption.

Unchanged transport and safety properties
-----------------------------------------
- Nine-bit USER2 capture and low/high frame-magic alignment lock.
- Independent A-tailpad and B-tailpad job delivery.
- SHA3-256T II=1 engines and Python digest verification.
- Independent device tags and extranonce spaces.
- PythonPool BitcoinIII endpoint stratum.pythonpool.dev:3357.
- No voltage operation and no 350 MHz restore path.

Physical qualification
----------------------
- 5-minute canary: A=199 accepted, B=175 accepted.
- 60-minute soak: A=1,181 accepted, B=1,187 accepted.
- Combined: A=1,380, B=1,362, rejected=0, digest mismatches=0.
- Final setup WNS: +0.010 ns.
- Final hold WHS: +0.010 ns.
- Maximum reported FPGA temperature: 30.133 C.
- No 650 MHz wall-power observation or efficiency claim.

The published miner retains the visible BitcoinIII 1% developer-fee policy.
That host-side change does not alter the qualified hardware image or evidence.
