JCM33 dual-alignment 550 MHz hardware-qualified release

Changes from the validated 525 MHz image
----------------------------------------
- Engine/MMCM output is exactly 550 MHz:
  200 MHz input * 5.500 / 2.000 = 550 MHz, VCO 1100 MHz.
- Implementation uses Vivado 2026.1 strategy
  Performance_ExplorePostRoutePhysOpt through post-route physical optimization.
- Setup and hold slack must both be nonnegative before the final uncompressed
  bitstream is emitted or any FPGA is programmed.
- The runner's offline-test path references the packaged 550 MHz test file.
- The BitcoinIII host miner implements a transparent 1% time-based developer
  rotation to the documented BitcoinIII address. Developer pool workers use
  the visible -DEVFEE suffix. FJAR policy is unchanged.

Unchanged transport and safety properties
-----------------------------------------
- Nine-bit USER2 capture and low/high frame-magic alignment lock.
- Independent A-tailpad and B-tailpad job delivery.
- SHA3-256T II=1 engines and Python digest verification.
- Independent device tags and extranonce spaces.
- PythonPool BitcoinIII endpoint stratum.pythonpool.dev:3357.
- No voltage operation and no 350 MHz restore path.

Physical qualification
----------------------
- 15-minute run: A=246 accepted, B=232 accepted.
- 60-minute soak: A=388 accepted, B=418 accepted.
- Combined: A=634, B=650, rejected=0, digest mismatches=0.
- Final setup WNS: +0.128 ns.
- Final hold WHS: +0.010 ns.
- Maximum reported FPGA temperature: 28.641 C.
- Whole-system wall power: 114 W including water pump and fans.

The host-side developer-fee policy was added after hardware qualification. It
does not change the qualified bitstream, FPGA work frames, digest validation,
clock tree, implementation result, or timing evidence.
